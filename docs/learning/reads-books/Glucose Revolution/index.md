# Glucose Revolution

*Jessie Inchauspé*

Inchauspé's claim is narrower than the marketing around it suggests: not average blood sugar or diabetes risk, but the shape of the glucose curve after a meal, and how ten specific, low-friction changes to food order, pairing, and timing can flatten that curve without changing what you actually eat. Broken into 11 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read covering the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — later chunks assume the earlier ones.

## Concepts

1. [The Glucose Curve, Not the Average](01%20-%20The%20Glucose%20Curve%2C%20Not%20the%20Average.md) — why the shape of your glucose response matters more than the total
2. [The Spike-Crash Mechanism](02%20-%20The%20Spike-Crash%20Mechanism.md) — how an insulin overshoot turns a spike into the crash that drives cravings
3. [Hack 1 — Eat Foods in the Right Order](03%20-%20Hack%201%20-%20Eat%20Foods%20in%20the%20Right%20Order.md) — vegetables and protein first, starches and sugar last
4. [Hack 2 — Vinegar Before You Eat](04%20-%20Hack%202%20-%20Vinegar%20Before%20You%20Eat.md) — a cheap, modestly-supported way to blunt a spike before it starts
5. [Hack 3 — Stop the Glucose Gremlins](05%20-%20Hack%203%20-%20Stop%20the%20Glucose%20Gremlins.md) — why sugar eaten alone spikes harder than sugar eaten with company
6. [Hack 4 — Savory Breakfast Over Sweet](06%20-%20Hack%204%20-%20Savory%20Breakfast%20Over%20Sweet.md) — how the first meal of the day sets the pattern for the rest of it
7. [Hack 5 — Move After You Eat](07%20-%20Hack%205%20-%20Move%20After%20You%20Eat.md) — a ten-minute walk gives muscles a second, insulin-free route to clear glucose
8. [Hack 6 — Put Clothes on Your Carbs](08%20-%20Hack%206%20-%20Put%20Clothes%20on%20Your%20Carbs.md) — the unifying principle underneath several of the other hacks
9. [Beyond Diabetes — Skin, Hormones, and Cravings](09%20-%20Beyond%20Diabetes%20-%20Skin%2C%20Hormones%2C%20and%20Cravings.md) — why the book's audience is metabolically healthy readers, not just diabetics
10. [The Contested Science of "Flatter Is Better"](10%20-%20The%20Contested%20Science%20of%20Flatter%20Is%20Better.md) — where the book's confident tone outruns the actual evidence
11. [Steady Glucose as the Real Lever](11%20-%20Steady%20Glucose%20as%20the%20Real%20Lever.md) — closing synthesis: managing the curve instead of counting calories
