# Rich Dad Poor Dad
*Robert Kiyosaki*

Broken into 8 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — later chunks assume the earlier ones.

## Concepts

1. [The Two Dads](01%20-%20The%20Two%20Dads.md) — the two father figures whose contrasting beliefs structure the whole book
2. [The Rich Don't Work for Money](02%20-%20The%20Rich%20Don't%20Work%20for%20Money.md) — how unexamined fear and desire keep people trading hours for a paycheck
3. [Assets vs Liabilities](03%20-%20Assets%20vs%20Liabilities.md) — the one definition the entire book's advice rests on
4. [The Rat Race Loop](04%20-%20The%20Rat%20Race%20Loop.md) — why raises alone don't build wealth, and what breaks the cycle
5. [Mind Your Own Business](05%20-%20Mind%20Your%20Own%20Business.md) — the difference between your job title and your actual asset column
6. [Pay Yourself First](06%20-%20Pay%20Yourself%20First.md) — putting your own asset column ahead of every bill, even under pressure
7. [Working to Learn, Not to Earn](07%20-%20Working%20to%20Learn%2C%20Not%20to%20Earn.md) — choosing early career moves for the skills they build, not the paycheck
8. [The Book's Reception and Legacy](08%20-%20The%20Book's%20Reception%20and%20Legacy.md) — what's verified, what's disputed, and how to weigh the book's influence against its critics
