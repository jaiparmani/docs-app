# The 4-Hour Workweek

*Timothy Ferriss*

Ferriss's pitch is that most work expands to fill the time given to it, and most of what feels urgent isn't actually important — so the fix is a deliberate program of elimination, automation, and outsourcing that frees time now rather than deferring it to a retirement decades away. Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read covering the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — the DEAL framework chunk sets up the ones that follow it.

## Concepts

1. [Parkinson's Law and the Design of Work](01%20-%20Parkinson's%20Law%20and%20the%20Design%20of%20Work.md) — why a task given eight hours takes eight hours
2. [The DEAL Framework](02%20-%20The%20DEAL%20Framework.md) — the four-part structure the rest of the book hangs on
3. [Elimination — The 80/20 Principle Applied Ruthlessly](03%20-%20Elimination%20-%20The%2080-20%20Principle%20Applied%20Ruthlessly.md) — cutting the clients and tasks that cost more than they're worth
4. [The Low-Information Diet](04%20-%20The%20Low-Information%20Diet.md) — treating news and inputs as a cost, not a virtue
5. [Batching and the End of Constant Interruption](05%20-%20Batching%20and%20the%20End%20of%20Constant%20Interruption.md) — checking email on your schedule instead of everyone else's
6. [Automation — Outsourcing to Virtual Assistants](06%20-%20Automation%20-%20Outsourcing%20to%20Virtual%20Assistants.md) — removing yourself as the bottleneck
7. [Liberation — Mini-Retirements](07%20-%20Liberation%20-%20Mini-Retirements.md) — taking freedom now, in pieces, instead of saving it for sixty-five
8. [Testing a Muse Business Before You Quit](08%20-%20Testing%20a%20Muse%20Business%20Before%20You%20Quit.md) — validating demand cheaply before committing
9. [Fear-Setting — What's the Worst That Could Happen](09%20-%20Fear-Setting%20-%20What's%20the%20Worst%20That%20Could%20Happen.md) — the exercise that later became his TED talk
10. [The Dated Playbook — Why Some of This Doesn't Transfer Today](10%20-%20The%20Dated%20Playbook%20-%20Why%20Some%20of%20This%20Doesn't%20Transfer%20Today.md) — what's aged out of a 2007 book and what hasn't
