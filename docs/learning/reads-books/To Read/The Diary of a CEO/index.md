# The Diary of a CEO
*Steven Bartlett*

Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Bartlett built the *Diary of a CEO* podcast by interviewing hundreds of entrepreneurs, scientists, athletes, and psychologists, then distilled recurring patterns from those conversations into this book's "33 laws." Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in any order.

## Concepts

1. [The Law of Category Kings](01%20-%20The%20Law%20of%20Category%20Kings.md) — why owning a category beats competing inside one
2. [Founder-Market Fit](02%20-%20Founder-Market%20Fit.md) — matching your specific history to the specific problem you're solving
3. [Self-Belief Under Uncertainty](03%20-%20Self-Belief%20Under%20Uncertainty.md) — holding conviction before the market has proven you right
4. [The Law of 100 Rejections](04%20-%20The%20Law%20of%20100%20Rejections.md) — reframing "no" as a numbers game, not a verdict
5. [Compounding — Consistency Over Intensity](05%20-%20Compounding%20-%20Consistency%20Over%20Intensity.md) — why showing up beats going hard
6. [The Law of the Few](06%20-%20The%20Law%20of%20the%20Few.md) — a small trusted circle beats a large shallow network
7. [Emotional Regulation as a Business Skill](07%20-%20Emotional%20Regulation%20as%20a%20Business%20Skill.md) — why managing your own state is a leadership skill, not a footnote
8. [The Comfort Trap After Early Success](08%20-%20The%20Comfort%20Trap%20After%20Early%20Success.md) — the danger success itself introduces
9. [Attention as the Real Battleground](09%20-%20Attention%20as%20the%20Real%20Battleground.md) — why distribution is a skill separate from having something good to say
10. [The Podcast-to-Book Tension](10%20-%20The%20Podcast-to-Book%20Tension.md) — reading a 33-law compilation for what kind of evidence each law actually rests on
