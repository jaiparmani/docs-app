# Daring Greatly
*Brené Brown*

Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — later chunks build on the framework the earlier ones establish.

## Concepts

1. [The Man in the Arena](01%20-%20The%20Man%20in%20the%20Arena.md) — the title's source, and why vulnerability is the arena, not the risk before it
2. [Debunking the Vulnerability Myths](02%20-%20Debunking%20the%20Vulnerability%20Myths.md) — the four beliefs people use as permission slips to avoid being seen
3. [A Decade of Grounded Theory](03%20-%20A%20Decade%20of%20Grounded%20Theory.md) — the qualitative research the book's popular-audience frameworks are actually built on
4. [The Scarcity Culture of Never Enough](04%20-%20The%20Scarcity%20Culture%20of%20Never%20Enough.md) — the cultural backdrop that makes being seen feel so risky
5. [Shame Is Not Guilt](05%20-%20Shame%20Is%20Not%20Guilt.md) — "I did something bad" versus "I am bad," and why the difference predicts opposite behavior
6. [Foreboding Joy](06%20-%20Foreboding%20Joy.md) — the habit of dress-rehearsing tragedy instead of just feeling a good moment
7. [Perfectionism as Armor](07%20-%20Perfectionism%20as%20Armor.md) — the twenty-ton shield that promises protection and delivers isolation
8. [Numbing and the Other Shields](08%20-%20Numbing%20and%20the%20Other%20Shields.md) — why you can't selectively numb pain without numbing joy too
9. [Vulnerability in Leadership](09%20-%20Vulnerability%20in%20Leadership.md) — the myth that admitting uncertainty undermines authority
10. [Wholehearted Parenting](10%20-%20Wholehearted%20Parenting.md) — kids learn shame resilience by watching, not by being told
