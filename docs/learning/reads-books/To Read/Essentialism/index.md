# Essentialism
*Greg McKeown*

McKeown's central claim: most people's real problem isn't too little time, it's the undisciplined pursuit of more — saying yes to too much because each request, evaluated alone, seems worth doing. The fix is "less but better": ruthlessly select the vital few things that matter and systematically eliminate the rest. Broken into 12 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — the Explore/Eliminate/Execute framework in chunk 04 organizes everything that follows it.

## Concepts

1. [The Essentialist vs Nonessentialist Mindset](01%20-%20The%20Essentialist%20vs%20Nonessentialist%20Mindset.md) — the three beliefs that separate reacting to everything from choosing deliberately
2. [Less But Better - The Disciplined Pursuit of Less](02%20-%20Less%20But%20Better%20-%20The%20Disciplined%20Pursuit%20of%20Less.md) — the central thesis, and why "priorities" used to be singular
3. [The Paradox of Success](03%20-%20The%20Paradox%20of%20Success.md) — why doing well at something is what makes it hardest to keep doing well
4. [Explore, Eliminate, Execute - The Framework](04%20-%20Explore%2C%20Eliminate%2C%20Execute%20-%20The%20Framework.md) — the organizing structure the rest of the book hangs on
5. [Escape to Think - Protecting Space to Explore](05%20-%20Escape%20to%20Think%20-%20Protecting%20Space%20to%20Explore.md) — why clarity requires stepping away from the busyness, not pushing through it
6. [Sleep - The Essentialist's Asset](06%20-%20Sleep%20-%20The%20Essentialist's%20Asset.md) — protecting rest as the precondition for good judgment, not a reward for it
7. [The Case for Play](07%20-%20The%20Case%20for%20Play.md) — why the activity with no obvious output is what broadens what you can even see
8. [The 90 Percent Rule](08%20-%20The%2090%20Percent%20Rule.md) — the extreme selection criterion built to filter out the merely good
9. [The Graceful "No"](09%20-%20The%20Graceful%20No.md) — separating the request from the relationship so elimination is actually possible
10. [Uncommitting - Escaping the Sunk-Cost Trap](10%20-%20Uncommitting%20-%20Escaping%20the%20Sunk-Cost%20Trap.md) — the reverse pilot as a way to test whether an old commitment still earns its place
11. [Buffer - Preparing for the Unexpected](11%20-%20Buffer%20-%20Preparing%20for%20the%20Unexpected.md) — why the vital few things still need slack, not just selection
12. [The Essentialist Life - From Doing to Being](12%20-%20The%20Essentialist%20Life%20-%20From%20Doing%20to%20Being.md) — closing synthesis of the whole book
