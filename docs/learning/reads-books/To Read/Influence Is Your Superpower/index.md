# Influence Is Your Superpower
*Zoe Chance*

Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Chance teaches Yale's most popular course on influence, and this book reframes persuasion away from the lever-pulling, extract-compliance tradition and toward "getting to yes" in ways that leave both people better off. Read them in order — later chunks build on the brain science and the asking mindset the early ones establish.

## Concepts

1. [Gator Brain vs. Judge Brain](01%20-%20Gator%20Brain%20vs.%20Judge%20Brain.md) — why influence has to work on instinct before it works on logic
2. [The Path of Least Resistance](02%20-%20The%20Path%20of%20Least%20Resistance.md) — ease beats motivation, price, and quality combined
3. [The Kindly Brontosaurus](03%20-%20The%20Kindly%20Brontosaurus.md) — why most people ask too little, too rarely, and give up too soon
4. [The Magic Question](04%20-%20The%20Magic%20Question.md) — turning "me vs. you" into "us vs. the problem"
5. [Anticipating Resistance](05%20-%20Anticipating%20Resistance.md) — redirecting an objection instead of pushing back against it
6. [Framing: Monumental, Manageable, Mysterious](06%20-%20Framing%20-%20Monumental%2C%20Manageable%2C%20Mysterious.md) — the same idea, three different doors into someone's attention
7. [Charm Is Attention, Not Performance](07%20-%20Charm%20Is%20Attention%20Not%20Performance.md) — why warmth outpredicts competence in whether people say yes
8. [Playfulness as an Influence Tool](08%20-%20Playfulness%20as%20an%20Influence%20Tool.md) — lowering the emotional stakes of an ask by not taking yourself too seriously
9. [Saying No Is Also Influence](09%20-%20Saying%20No%20Is%20Also%20Influence.md) — why a skilled "no" is part of the same skill set as a skilled "ask"
10. [Defense Against the Dark Arts](10%20-%20Defense%20Against%20the%20Dark%20Arts.md) — where genuine influence ends and manipulation begins, and how this book sits next to Cialdini's *Influence*
