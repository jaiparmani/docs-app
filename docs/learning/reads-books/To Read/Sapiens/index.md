# Sapiens
*Yuval Noah Harari*

Broken into 13 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a way to apply the thinking, and a question to sit with. The chunks roughly follow the book's own arc — Cognitive Revolution, Agricultural Revolution, the unification of humankind through money/empire/religion, then the Scientific Revolution — so reading them in order tracks the book's own structure.

## Concepts

1. [The Cognitive Revolution and the Power of Gossip](01%20-%20The%20Cognitive%20Revolution%20and%20the%20Power%20of%20Gossip.md) — how a new kind of language broke the 150-person ceiling on group size
2. [Shared Fictions, the Engine of Cooperation](02%20-%20Shared%20Fictions%2C%20the%20Engine%20of%20Cooperation.md) — the single idea the whole book hangs on
3. [Sapiens and the Other Humans](03%20-%20Sapiens%20and%20the%20Other%20Humans.md) — what happened to Neanderthals and the other human species sapiens once shared the planet with
4. [The Forager's Life Reconsidered](04%20-%20The%20Forager's%20Life%20Reconsidered.md) — why the pre-agricultural life wasn't the miserable existence it's often assumed to be
5. [The Agricultural Revolution as History's Biggest Fraud](05%20-%20The%20Agricultural%20Revolution%20as%20History's%20Biggest%20Fraud.md) — Harari's most provocative claim, and the mechanics behind it
6. [The Luxury Trap](06%20-%20The%20Luxury%20Trap.md) — why conveniences quietly become inescapable requirements
7. [Imagined Orders Are Enforced, Not Natural](07%20-%20Imagined%20Orders%20Are%20Enforced%2C%20Not%20Natural.md) — Hammurabi's Code and the Declaration of Independence as two versions of the same trick
8. [Money, the Most Universal Fiction](08%20-%20Money%2C%20the%20Most%20Universal%20Fiction.md) — the shared story that gets strangers who agree on nothing else to cooperate
9. [Empire as a Universal Order](09%20-%20Empire%20as%20a%20Universal%20Order.md) — why most "native" traditions are quietly downstream of some earlier conquest
10. [Religion and Ideology as Universal Orders](10%20-%20Religion%20and%20Ideology%20as%20Universal%20Orders.md) — why Harari calls liberalism and communism religions too
11. [The Scientific Revolution and the Discovery of Ignorance](11%20-%20The%20Scientific%20Revolution%20and%20the%20Discovery%20of%20Ignorance.md) — why "we don't know" was the real breakthrough
12. [Capitalism and the Discovery of the Future](12%20-%20Capitalism%20and%20the%20Discovery%20of%20the%20Future.md) — how trusting the future into existence became an economic engine
13. [Where Scholars Push Back](13%20-%20Where%20Scholars%20Push%20Back.md) — an honest look at where historians and anthropologists think the book overreaches
