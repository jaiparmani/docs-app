# Beyond the Syllabus
*Ankur Warikoo*

A 2025 release aimed at teenagers rather than Warikoo's usual working-adult audience, built around the same direct, no-platitudes voice he uses in his career and money content. These notes stay thematic and general — the book is too new and too niche for confident claims about specific chapters or passages, so the goal here is to capture the book's likely approach and register rather than its exact contents.

## Concepts

1. [What School Leaves Out](01%20-%20What%20School%20Leaves%20Out.md) — the book's core premise: marks are measured, life skills aren't
2. [Money Basics Before the Stakes Get Real](02%20-%20Money%20Basics%20Before%20the%20Stakes%20Get%20Real.md) — why financial literacy should start before the first real paycheck
3. [The Comparison Trap in a Feed-Driven World](03%20-%20The%20Comparison%20Trap%20in%20a%20Feed-Driven%20World.md) — how social media turns other people's highlight reels into a spending and self-worth benchmark
4. [Confidence Is a Skill, Not a Performance](04%20-%20Confidence%20Is%20a%20Skill%2C%20Not%20a%20Performance.md) — the gap between looking confident and being able to act despite fear
5. [Peer Pressure and the Cost of Fitting In](05%20-%20Peer%20Pressure%20and%20the%20Cost%20of%20Fitting%20In.md) — why belonging and identity are worth naming as separate, competing needs
6. [Marks Are a Measure, Not the Whole Story](06%20-%20Marks%20Are%20a%20Measure%2C%20Not%20the%20Whole%20Story.md) — decoupling self-worth and future prospects from exam results
7. [Talking About Hard Things](07%20-%20Talking%20About%20Hard%20Things.md) — communication and relationship skills for a generation that negotiates most of its conflict through a screen
8. [A Pivot in Audience, Not in Voice](08%20-%20A%20Pivot%20in%20Audience%2C%20Not%20in%20Voice.md) — what changes and what doesn't when Warikoo writes for teenagers instead of adults
