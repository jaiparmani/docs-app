# 101 Essays That Will Change The Way You Think

*Brianna Wiest*

This is a compilation of short, standalone essays — many originally published individually online before being collected into one book — so there's no single sustained argument running chapter to chapter the way a traditional nonfiction book builds one thesis. Instead, the chunks below group the essays by recurring theme rather than pretend it's a unified argument; read them in any order, as a browsable collection rather than a cover-to-cover case.

## Concepts

1. [What You Think of Yourself Outweighs What Others Think](01%20-%20What%20You%20Think%20of%20Yourself%20Outweighs%20What%20Others%20Think.md) — why your self-concept filters every external opinion, not the other way around
2. [Self-Sabotage Is Usually Self-Protection](02%20-%20Self-Sabotage%20Is%20Usually%20Self-Protection.md) — reframing sabotage as an old survival strategy, not a character flaw
3. [Healing Is Not the Same as Coping](03%20-%20Healing%20Is%20Not%20the%20Same%20as%20Coping.md) — why functioning well isn't proof something's actually been resolved
4. [Emotional Intelligence Is a Skill, Not a Trait](04%20-%20Emotional%20Intelligence%20Is%20a%20Skill%2C%20Not%20a%20Trait.md) — treating naming and managing feelings as a practiced skill, not an innate gift
5. [You Are What You Repeatedly Do](05%20-%20You%20Are%20What%20You%20Repeatedly%20Do.md) — how self-trust is built or eroded through small kept or broken promises to yourself
6. [Closure Is Something You Give Yourself](06%20-%20Closure%20Is%20Something%20You%20Give%20Yourself.md) — why waiting for someone else's explanation keeps you stuck
7. [Alone Is Not the Same as Lonely](07%20-%20Alone%20Is%20Not%20the%20Same%20as%20Lonely.md) — why loneliness is about disconnection, not headcount
8. [The Hard Season Is Not a Waste](08%20-%20The%20Hard%20Season%20Is%20Not%20a%20Waste.md) — reframing difficult periods as necessary material, not pure loss
9. [A Book Built to Be Dipped Into, Not Devoured](09%20-%20A%20Book%20Built%20to%20Be%20Dipped%20Into%2C%20Not%20Devoured.md) — how the essay-compilation structure should change the way you read it
