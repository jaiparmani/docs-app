# Born a Crime
*Trevor Noah*

A narrative memoir, not a framework book — there's no system to extract, just a specific childhood told mostly through the choices of Noah's mother, Patricia, growing up mixed-race in and after apartheid South Africa. Broken into 10 concepts, each built around a real episode or thread from the book, covering the core idea, why it matters, the actual scene it comes from, how to carry it forward, and a question to sit with. Read in roughly the order the book unfolds them.

## Concepts

1. [Born a Crime](01%20-%20Born%20a%20Crime.md) — why the title isn't a metaphor: his birth itself broke the law
2. [Thrown From a Moving Taxi](02%20-%20Thrown%20From%20a%20Moving%20Taxi.md) — the opening scene, and what it says about his mother's instincts
3. [Language as Camouflage](03%20-%20Language%20as%20Camouflage.md) — how fluency across tongues let him cross lines apartheid built to be uncrossable
4. [Three Churches Every Sunday](04%20-%20Three%20Churches%20Every%20Sunday.md) — Patricia's faith and the relentless, deliberate way she raised him
5. [The Mathematics of Race](05%20-%20The%20Mathematics%20of%20Race.md) — apartheid's bureaucratic absurdity, and a body that didn't fit its boxes
6. [Choosing a Table](06%20-%20Choosing%20a%20Table.md) — high school lunchrooms as a map of a country still segregating itself
7. [Two Worlds, One Family](07%20-%20Two%20Worlds%2C%20One%20Family.md) — poverty, Soweto, and the resourcefulness it demanded
8. [The Hustle](08%20-%20The%20Hustle.md) — the CD-piracy business and what it taught him about value
9. [Abel](09%20-%20Abel.md) — the stepfather, the charm, and the slow arrival of danger
10. [My Mother's Life](10%20-%20My%20Mother's%20Life.md) — the shooting, and the faith that outlasted it
