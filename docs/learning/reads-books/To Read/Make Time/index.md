# Make Time

*Jake Knapp and John Zeratsky*

From the creators of Google Ventures' "design sprint" methodology, a deliberately tactical (not philosophical) system for protecting one thing a day from the pull of infinite feeds and inboxes. Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read covering the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them roughly in order — the first three build the daily framework the rest hangs on.

## Concepts

1. [The Daily Framework — Highlight, Laser, Energize, Reflect](01%20-%20The%20Daily%20Framework%20-%20Highlight%2C%20Laser%2C%20Energize%2C%20Reflect.md) — the four-step loop that repeats every single day
2. [Choosing Your Highlight — Urgency, Satisfaction, or Joy](02%20-%20Choosing%20Your%20Highlight%20-%20Urgency%2C%20Satisfaction%2C%20or%20Joy.md) — three quick lenses for naming the one thing that matters today
3. [Clock Blocking — Turning the Highlight into Calendar Time](03%20-%20Clock%20Blocking%20-%20Turning%20the%20Highlight%20into%20Calendar%20Time.md) — why an intention needs a defended slot on your calendar
4. [Infinity Pools — Apps Built to Never Run Dry](04%20-%20Infinity%20Pools%20-%20Apps%20Built%20to%20Never%20Run%20Dry.md) — why some distractions aren't a willpower problem at all
5. [The Distraction-Free Phone](05%20-%20The%20Distraction-Free%20Phone.md) — redesigning your home screen so the easy tap isn't the pool
6. [Taming Notifications and the Morning Check-In](06%20-%20Taming%20Notifications%20and%20the%20Morning%20Check-In.md) — protecting the highest-willpower part of your day
7. [The Two Coffee Method — Front-Loading the Hard Work](07%20-%20The%20Two%20Coffee%20Method%20-%20Front-Loading%20the%20Hard%20Work.md) — using your morning ritual as a trigger to start, not to check messages
8. [Energize — Fueling the Body to Fuel the Mind](08%20-%20Energize%20-%20Fueling%20the%20Body%20to%20Fuel%20the%20Mind.md) — why focus is a physical capacity before it's a mental one
9. [Reflect — The Nightly Tune-Up](09%20-%20Reflect%20-%20The%20Nightly%20Tune-Up.md) — the short review that turns the system into a personal experiment
10. [Make Time vs. Essentialism — Tactics vs. Philosophy](10%20-%20Make%20Time%20vs.%20Essentialism%20-%20Tactics%20vs.%20Philosophy.md) — two different failure modes, two different books
