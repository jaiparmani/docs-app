# Get It Done
*Ayelet Fishbach*

Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Fishbach is a motivation science researcher, and the book's central claim is that motivation is not a fixed trait or a willpower reserve — it's a structural property of how a goal is set up, built from her own lab's experimental research rather than synthesized anecdotes. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific study from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — later chunks build on the framing established early on.

## Concepts

1. [Motivation Is Structure, Not a Trait](01%20-%20Motivation%20Is%20Structure%2C%20Not%20a%20Trait.md) — the book's central reframe: the goal's architecture does the work, not your character
2. [Specific, Moderately Difficult Goals Beat "Do Your Best"](02%20-%20Specific%2C%20Moderately%20Difficult%20Goals%20Beat%20Do%20Your%20Best.md) — why vague targets and impossible stretch goals both underperform
3. [Means vs. Ends — Enjoying the Journey vs. Fixating on the Outcome](03%20-%20Means%20vs%20Ends%20-%20Enjoying%20the%20Journey%20vs%20Fixating%20on%20the%20Outcome.md) — why caring only about the destination makes the trip unbearable
4. [Tracking Progress — "How Far I've Come" vs. "How Far I Have to Go"](04%20-%20Tracking%20Progress%20-%20How%20Far%20I%27ve%20Come%20vs%20How%20Far%20I%20Have%20to%20Go.md) — the framing that motivates flips halfway through
5. [When Monitoring Backfires — The Licensing Effect](05%20-%20When%20Monitoring%20Backfires%20-%20The%20Licensing%20Effect.md) — how seeing your own progress can talk you into quitting early
6. [The Zeigarnik Effect — the Pull of Unfinished Business](06%20-%20The%20Zeigarnik%20Effect%20-%20The%20Pull%20of%20Unfinished%20Business.md) — why an open task haunts you and a closed one vanishes from memory
7. [Curiosity Gaps — Turning a Task Into a Mystery](07%20-%20Curiosity%20Gaps%20-%20Turning%20a%20Task%20Into%20a%20Mystery.md) — a renewable fuel source that has nothing to do with reward
8. [Social Support — Helps as Encouragement, Hurts as Surveillance](08%20-%20Social%20Support%20-%20Helps%20as%20Encouragement%2C%20Hurts%20as%20Surveillance.md) — why the same helpful partner can boost or sabotage you
9. [Temptation Bundling and Structuring Your Environment](09%20-%20Temptation%20Bundling%20and%20Structuring%20Your%20Environment.md) — designing the choice instead of resisting it
10. [Pursuing Multiple Goals — Conflict, Facilitation, and Goal Shielding](10%20-%20Pursuing%20Multiple%20Goals%20-%20Conflict%2C%20Facilitation%2C%20and%20Goal%20Shielding.md) — how your mind protects one goal by muting the others
