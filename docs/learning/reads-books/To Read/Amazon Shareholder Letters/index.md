# Amazon Shareholder Letters

*Jeff Bezos*

This is compiled primary-source material, not a conventional book — Bezos's actual annual letters to Amazon shareholders, written from 1997 through his final letters before stepping down as CEO. Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each organized around a recurring theme that appears across the letters rather than a chapter of the original text. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the letters, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with — read them by theme rather than in strict order, since the letters themselves are a reference to dip into rather than a single developing argument.

## Concepts

1. [It's All About the Long Term](01%20-%20It's%20All%20About%20the%20Long%20Term.md) — the 1997 founding letter reprinted every year for two decades
2. [Customer Obsession as the North Star](02%20-%20Customer%20Obsession%20as%20the%20North%20Star.md) — why being customer-focused beats being competitor-focused
3. [The Day 1 Philosophy](03%20-%20The%20Day%201%20Philosophy.md) — the explicit warning against stasis, irrelevance, decline, and death
4. [The Regret Minimization Framework](04%20-%20The%20Regret%20Minimization%20Framework.md) — the age-80 test Bezos used to justify starting Amazon
5. [High-Velocity Decisions and Disagree and Commit](05%20-%20High-Velocity%20Decisions%20and%20Disagree%20and%20Commit.md) — moving fast on 70% information, and committing even when you disagree
6. [Type 1 and Type 2 Decisions](06%20-%20Type%201%20and%20Type%202%20Decisions.md) — sorting one-way doors from two-way doors before deciding how carefully to decide
7. [Willingness to Be Misunderstood](07%20-%20Willingness%20to%20Be%20Misunderstood.md) — why real long-term bets look wrong from the outside until they don't
8. [The Two-Pizza Team](08%20-%20The%20Two-Pizza%20Team.md) — keeping teams small enough to stay fast as the company scales
9. [The Invention Machine](09%20-%20The%20Invention%20Machine.md) — why most bets are supposed to fail
10. [Reading Primary Source Material](10%20-%20Reading%20Primary%20Source%20Material.md) — what you gain and lose reading Bezos's own words instead of someone else's account
