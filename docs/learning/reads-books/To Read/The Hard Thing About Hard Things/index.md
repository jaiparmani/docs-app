# The Hard Thing About Hard Things
*Ben Horowitz*

Broken into 11 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Drawn almost entirely from Horowitz's own run as CEO of Loudcloud and then Opsware — a company that came within weeks of running out of cash during the dot-com crash, sold off its core business to survive, and eventually sold the rest to HP for $1.6 billion. Read them in order — later chunks assume the earlier ones.

## Concepts

1. [This Time With Feeling — Surviving the Crash](01%20-%20This%20Time%20With%20Feeling%20-%20Surviving%20the%20Crash.md) — how Loudcloud's near-death wasn't one bad moment but a compounding one
2. [The Struggle](02%20-%20The%20Struggle.md) — naming the sustained psychological toll of running a company under existential threat
3. [Wartime CEO, Peacetime CEO](03%20-%20Wartime%20CEO%2C%20Peacetime%20CEO.md) — why the leadership style that works in growth mode can sink you in crisis mode
4. [Tell It Like It Is — Ben's Rule for CEOs](04%20-%20Tell%20It%20Like%20It%20Is%20-%20Bens%20Rule%20for%20CEOs.md) — why spin is the fastest way to lose your own organization's trust
5. [The Right Way to Lay People Off](05%20-%20The%20Right%20Way%20to%20Lay%20People%20Off.md) — doing a brutal thing without destroying the culture in the process
6. [Firing an Executive](06%20-%20Firing%20an%20Executive.md) — why the standard advice for firing employees doesn't apply the same way at the top
7. [Take Care of the People, the Products, and the Profits — in That Order](07%20-%20Take%20Care%20of%20the%20People%2C%20the%20Products%2C%20and%20the%20Profits%20-%20in%20That%20Order.md) — the priority ordering Horowitz says most CEOs get backward
8. [The One-on-One](08%20-%20The%20One-on-One.md) — why it has to be the employee's meeting, not the manager's
9. [Hire for Strength, Not for Lack of Weakness](09%20-%20Hire%20for%20Strength%2C%20Not%20for%20Lack%20of%20Weakness.md) — why hiring-by-committee quietly selects for mediocrity
10. [The Freaky Friday Management Technique](10%20-%20The%20Freaky%20Friday%20Management%20Technique.md) — solving a turf war by literally swapping the two people fighting it
11. [The EDS Deal — Saving the Company](11%20-%20The%20EDS%20Deal%20-%20Saving%20the%20Company.md) — the negotiation that let Loudcloud survive by selling the very business it was built on
