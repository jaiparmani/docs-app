# Start with Why
*Simon Sinek*

Sinek's central claim, built out from his widely watched TED talk: the organizations and leaders that inspire genuine loyalty communicate purpose first — Why, then How, then What — while most communicate in the opposite order. Broken into 9 concepts, one per chunk, each a 4-6 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — the later chunks (diffusion of innovation, the split) build on the Golden Circle introduced first.

## Concepts

1. [The Golden Circle](01%20-%20The%20Golden%20Circle.md) — Why, How, What, and the direction most organizations get backwards
2. [The Neuroscience of Why](02%20-%20The%20Neuroscience%20of%20Why.md) — why purpose-first messaging feels like a "gut decision" rather than a rational one
3. [Manipulation vs Inspiration](03%20-%20Manipulation%20vs%20Inspiration.md) — two ways to move people to act, and why only one earns loyalty
4. [The Apple Case Study](04%20-%20The%20Apple%20Case%20Study.md) — the book's flagship example of Why-first communication in practice
5. [The Wright Brothers vs. Samuel Pierpont Langley](05%20-%20The%20Wright%20Brothers%20vs.%20Samuel%20Pierpont%20Langley.md) — how the underdog beat the well-funded favorite to powered flight
6. [Martin Luther King and the Power of Belief](06%20-%20Martin%20Luther%20King%20and%20the%20Power%20of%20Belief.md) — why 250,000 people showed up for a man, not a program
7. [The Law of Diffusion of Innovation](07%20-%20The%20Law%20of%20Diffusion%20of%20Innovation.md) — why you need the believers before you can win the majority
8. [The Split](08%20-%20The%20Split.md) — what happens to Why when an organization outgrows its founder
9. [A Talk vs a Rigorous Business Case](09%20-%20A%20Talk%20vs%20a%20Rigorous%20Business%20Case.md) — an honest look at where the framework's evidence is thinner than its polish
