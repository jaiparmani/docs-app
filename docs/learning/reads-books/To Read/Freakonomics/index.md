# Freakonomics
*Steven Levitt and Stephen Dubner*

Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — later chunks build on the incentive-thinking established early on.

## Concepts

1. [Incentives Are the Root of Everything](01%20-%20Incentives%20Are%20the%20Root%20of%20Everything.md) — the three flavors of incentive that explain almost any behavior
2. [What Schoolteachers and Sumo Wrestlers Have in Common](02%20-%20What%20Schoolteachers%20and%20Sumo%20Wrestlers%20Have%20in%20Common.md) — how cheating leaves a statistical fingerprint
3. [Real Estate Agents and the Price of Information](03%20-%20Real%20Estate%20Agents%20and%20the%20Price%20of%20Information.md) — why the expert selling you advice doesn't act on it themselves
4. [How the Klan Lost Its Secrets](04%20-%20How%20the%20Klan%20Lost%20Its%20Secrets.md) — how exposing information destroyed an organization built on withholding it
5. [Why Drug Dealers Still Live With Their Moms](05%20-%20Why%20Drug%20Dealers%20Still%20Live%20With%20Their%20Moms.md) — a street gang's ledger reveals a corporate pay structure in disguise
6. [What Makes a Perfect Parent](06%20-%20What%20Makes%20a%20Perfect%20Parent.md) — why what parents do matters less than who parents are
7. [A Name Is a Signal, Not a Cause](07%20-%20A%20Name%20Is%20a%20Signal%2C%20Not%20a%20Cause.md) — what a first name can and can't tell you about a life
8. [The Abortion-Crime Hypothesis](08%20-%20The%20Abortion-Crime%20Hypothesis.md) — the book's most explosive argument about why crime fell in the 1990s
9. [A Famous Claim Under Fire](09%20-%20A%20Famous%20Claim%20Under%20Fire.md) — the methodological pushback that followed the abortion-crime argument
10. [Conventional Wisdom Deserves a Second Look](10%20-%20Conventional%20Wisdom%20Deserves%20a%20Second%20Look.md) — closing synthesis of the whole book
