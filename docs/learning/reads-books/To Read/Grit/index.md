# Grit
*Angela Duckworth*

Duckworth's central claim: grit — sustained passion and perseverance toward a long-term goal, not raw talent or momentary intensity — is a distinct, measurable trait that predicts achievement better than aptitude alone. Broken into 11 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — the second half builds on the four psychological assets introduced partway through.

## Concepts

1. [What Grit Really Means](01%20-%20What%20Grit%20Really%20Means.md) — why "passion" in Duckworth's sense means consistency over years, not intensity in the moment
2. [Effort Counts Twice](02%20-%20Effort%20Counts%20Twice.md) — the two-step equation that shows why effort outweighs talent
3. [The West Point Study](03%20-%20The%20West%20Point%20Study.md) — how a simple questionnaire out-predicted the Academy's own admissions formula
4. [The Grit Scale](04%20-%20The%20Grit%20Scale.md) — turning a fuzzy virtue into something you can actually measure
5. [Deliberate Practice and the Spelling Bee](05%20-%20Deliberate%20Practice%20and%20the%20Spelling%20Bee.md) — why gritty people practice differently, not just longer
6. [The Four Psychological Assets of Grit](06%20-%20The%20Four%20Psychological%20Assets%20of%20Grit.md) — interest, practice, purpose, and hope, and the order they tend to arrive in
7. [Interest Is Cultivated, Not Discovered](07%20-%20Interest%20Is%20Cultivated%2C%20Not%20Discovered.md) — why waiting for a passion to strike you is a bad strategy
8. [Purpose Beyond Yourself](08%20-%20Purpose%20Beyond%20Yourself.md) — the ingredient that turns a hobby into something you'll sustain for decades
9. [Hope and the Growth Mindset](09%20-%20Hope%20and%20the%20Growth%20Mindset.md) — the belief that keeps interest and purpose alive through setbacks
10. [The Hard Thing Rule](10%20-%20The%20Hard%20Thing%20Rule.md) — Duckworth's own family policy for building grit in kids (and yourself)
11. [A Culture of Grit](11%20-%20A%20Culture%20of%20Grit.md) — why the fastest way to get grittier might be to change who you surround yourself with
