# The Everything Store
*Brad Stone*

Stone's book is a reported history of Amazon's first two decades, not a founder's memoir — built from hundreds of interviews with current and former executives, competitors, and Bezos's own family, without his full authorized cooperation. Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a real episode the book documents rather than a management framework. Read them roughly in order — the first few establish the founding logic, the middle covers the tactics and bets, the last two cover the book's own reliability.

## Concepts

1. [The Regret Minimization Framework](01%20-%20The%20Regret%20Minimization%20Framework.md) — the decision tool Bezos used to quit a lucrative hedge-fund job
2. [Why Books, Specifically](02%20-%20Why%20Books%2C%20Specifically.md) — the unglamorous infrastructure reason books came first
3. [Get Big Fast and the Cash Crunch](03%20-%20Get%20Big%20Fast%20and%20the%20Cash%20Crunch.md) — the growth-over-profit bet that nearly killed the company
4. [The Gazelle Project](04%20-%20The%20Gazelle%20Project.md) — the internal program for squeezing small publishers
5. [The Diapers.com War](05%20-%20The%20Diapers.com%20War.md) — how Amazon priced a rival into selling
6. [Turning Internal Infrastructure Into AWS](06%20-%20Turning%20Internal%20Infrastructure%20Into%20AWS.md) — the accidental origin of Amazon's most profitable business
7. [The Empty Chair — Customer Obsession as Doctrine](07%20-%20The%20Empty%20Chair%20-%20Customer%20Obsession%20as%20Doctrine.md) — turning a value into an enforced ritual
8. [The Bezos Standard](08%20-%20The%20Bezos%20Standard.md) — narrative memos, brutal meetings, and the cost of a high bar
9. [Reported, Not Authorized](09%20-%20Reported%2C%20Not%20Authorized.md) — why this book reads nothing like a founder's memoir
10. [A Contested Record](10%20-%20A%20Contested%20Record.md) — Bezos's pushback, and how dated the book already is
