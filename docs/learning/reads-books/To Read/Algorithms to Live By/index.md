# Algorithms to Live By
*Brian Christian and Tom Griffiths*

Christian, a writer, and Griffiths, a cognitive scientist, argue that computer science has already solved formal versions of problems people face informally every day — and that the actual algorithms, not loose metaphors inspired by them, have specific, often counterintuitive answers worth borrowing directly. Broken into 11 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read, roughly following the book's own chapter structure. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with.

## Concepts

1. [The 37 Percent Rule and Optimal Stopping](01%20-%20The%2037%20Percent%20Rule%20and%20Optimal%20Stopping.md) — when to stop searching and commit, from apartment hunting to hiring to marriage
2. [Explore vs Exploit and the Multi-Armed Bandit](02%20-%20Explore%20vs%20Exploit%20and%20the%20Multi-Armed%20Bandit.md) — why the right ratio of trying new things to sticking with favorites depends on your time horizon
3. [Sorting and the Cost of Organizing](03%20-%20Sorting%20and%20the%20Cost%20of%20Organizing.md) — why alphabetizing a rarely-searched bookshelf is often a net loss
4. [Caching and the Logic of a Messy Desk](04%20-%20Caching%20and%20the%20Logic%20of%20a%20Messy%20Desk.md) — how a pile with recent things on top is closer to optimal than it looks
5. [Scheduling and the Cost of Switching Tasks](05%20-%20Scheduling%20and%20the%20Cost%20of%20Switching%20Tasks.md) — why "prioritize by urgency" is several different rules, and what thrashing costs you
6. [Bayes's Rule and Predicting How Long Things Will Last](06%20-%20Bayes's%20Rule%20and%20Predicting%20How%20Long%20Things%20Will%20Last.md) — why the right forecast depends on what kind of thing you're forecasting
7. [Overfitting and the Case for Simpler Rules](07%20-%20Overfitting%20and%20the%20Case%20for%20Simpler%20Rules.md) — why a blunt heuristic can beat a plan perfectly tailored to today
8. [Relaxation - Solving the Easier Problem First](08%20-%20Relaxation%20-%20Solving%20the%20Easier%20Problem%20First.md) — dropping a constraint on purpose to find out what you're actually optimizing for
9. [Randomness and the Power of Chance](09%20-%20Randomness%20and%20the%20Power%20of%20Chance.md) — when a coin flip is the mathematically correct move, not a cop-out
10. [Networking and the Etiquette of Backing Off](10%20-%20Networking%20and%20the%20Etiquette%20of%20Backing%20Off.md) — what TCP acknowledgment and exponential backoff say about conversation and follow-up
11. [Game Theory and the Limits of Cleverness](11%20-%20Game%20Theory%20and%20the%20Limits%20of%20Cleverness.md) — why some bad situations need a different game, not better play, and what kind of book this really is
