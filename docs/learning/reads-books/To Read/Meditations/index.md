# Meditations

*Marcus Aurelius*

Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. This isn't a linear argument — it's a private journal Marcus kept for himself, so the chunks are organized around the handful of Stoic themes he circles back to across all twelve books, not around a chapter-by-chapter structure the original doesn't have. Read them in any order, and come back to individual ones later rather than treating this as a one-time read-through.

## Concepts

1. [Reading a Private Notebook, Not a Treatise](01%20-%20Reading%20a%20Private%20Notebook%2C%20Not%20a%20Treatise.md) — why this book disappoints anyone expecting a modern argument, and its direct link to *The Obstacle Is the Way*
2. [The Dichotomy of Control](02%20-%20The%20Dichotomy%20of%20Control.md) — sorting what's "up to us" from what isn't
3. [The Discipline of Perception](03%20-%20The%20Discipline%20of%20Perception.md) — events are neutral; judgment is what adds the suffering
4. [Living According to Nature and Reason](04%20-%20Living%20According%20to%20Nature%20and%20Reason.md) — why acting well is coherence, not obedience
5. [Memento Mori and the Present Moment](05%20-%20Memento%20Mori%20and%20the%20Present%20Moment.md) — mortality as a clarifying deadline, not a morbid fact
6. [The View From Above](06%20-%20The%20View%20From%20Above.md) — zooming out until today's crisis shrinks to scale
7. [Duty and Service to the Commonwealth](07%20-%20Duty%20and%20Service%20to%20the%20Commonwealth.md) — why inner calm exists to serve others, not replace that duty
8. [Forgiving Others Their Faults](08%20-%20Forgiving%20Others%20Their%20Faults.md) — people do wrong from ignorance, not malice
9. [The Impermanence of Fame](09%20-%20The%20Impermanence%20of%20Fame.md) — why the most famous man of his era planned for being forgotten
10. [The Obstacle Is the Way](10%20-%20The%20Obstacle%20Is%20the%20Way.md) — the line that later gave Ryan Holiday's book its title
