# The Almanack of Naval Ravikant
*Eric Jorgenson (compiler)*

This is a curated compilation, not an originally authored narrative book — Jorgenson has assembled Naval Ravikant's tweets, a widely circulated podcast transcript, and public essays into a single volume with connective narration bridging fragments that were never written to sit next to each other. Broken into 12 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read, split across the book's two real halves: wealth (chunks 1-7) and happiness (chunks 8-12). Read them in order within each half; the two halves are otherwise fairly independent of each other.

## Concepts

### Wealth
1. [Wealth Is Owning Equity, Not Trading Time for Money](01%20-%20Wealth%20Is%20Owning%20Equity%2C%20Not%20Trading%20Time%20for%20Money.md) — why a salary has a ceiling that ownership doesn't
2. [Specific Knowledge](02%20-%20Specific%20Knowledge.md) — the untrainable expertise that feels like play to you and work to everyone else
3. [The Three Types of Leverage](03%20-%20The%20Three%20Types%20of%20Leverage.md) — labor and capital need permission, code and media don't
4. [Seek Wealth, Not Money or Status](04%20-%20Seek%20Wealth%2C%20Not%20Money%20or%20Status.md) — why status is a zero-sum game dressed up as ambition
5. [Judgment Is the Scarce Skill That Compounds](05%20-%20Judgment%20Is%20the%20Scarce%20Skill%20That%20Compounds.md) — what actually matters once leverage itself is cheap for everyone
6. [Play Long-Term Games With Long-Term People](06%20-%20Play%20Long-Term%20Games%20With%20Long-Term%20People.md) — why repeated relationships out-compound one-shot deals

### Happiness
