# Deep Work

*Cal Newport*

Broken into 12 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — later chunks assume the earlier ones.

## Concepts

1. [The Deep Work Hypothesis](01%20-%20The%20Deep%20Work%20Hypothesis.md) — why concentration is becoming both rarer and more valuable
2. [Deep Work Is Rare](02%20-%20Deep%20Work%20Is%20Rare.md) — why busyness became the stand-in for real productivity
3. [The Four Philosophies of Deep Work](03%20-%20The%20Four%20Philosophies%20of%20Deep%20Work.md) — matching a schedule structure to your actual constraints
4. [Ritualize Your Deep Work](04%20-%20Ritualize%20Your%20Deep%20Work.md) — removing the willpower cost of starting
5. [The Grand Gesture](05%20-%20The%20Grand%20Gesture.md) — using a big, deliberate disruption to unstick a stalled project
6. [Execute Like a Business](06%20-%20Execute%20Like%20a%20Business.md) — borrowing 4DX to actually follow through on the habit
7. [Be Lazy](07%20-%20Be%20Lazy.md) — why real downtime is what makes the next day's depth possible
8. [Don't Take Breaks from Focus, Take Breaks from Distraction](08%20-%20Don%27t%20Take%20Breaks%20from%20Focus%2C%20Take%20Breaks%20from%20Distraction.md) — training tolerance for boredom instead of relieving it
9. [Productive Meditation](09%20-%20Productive%20Meditation.md) — turning dead time into deliberate, sustained thinking
10. [The Any-Benefit Mindset vs. the Craftsman Approach](10%20-%20The%20Any-Benefit%20Mindset%20vs.%20the%20Craftsman%20Approach.md) — a real test for whether a tool earns its cost
11. [Fixed-Schedule Productivity](11%20-%20Fixed-Schedule%20Productivity.md) — letting a hard deadline force the prioritization you've been avoiding
12. [Become Hard to Reach](12%20-%20Become%20Hard%20to%20Reach.md) — why sender-centric email trains the world to interrupt you more
