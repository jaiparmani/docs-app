# Mindset
*Carol Dweck*

Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — later chunks build on the fixed/growth distinction the first two chunks establish.

## Concepts

1. [Fixed Mindset vs. Growth Mindset](01%20-%20Fixed%20Mindset%20vs.%20Growth%20Mindset.md) — the belief about ability that quietly shapes everything else
2. [How the Two Mindsets React to Failure and Challenge](02%20-%20How%20the%20Two%20Mindsets%20React%20to%20Failure%20and%20Challenge.md) — the four everyday moments where the difference shows up
3. [The Myth of Born Talent](03%20-%20The%20Myth%20of%20Born%20Talent.md) — why "naturals" like Darwin and Tolstoy started out ordinary
4. [CEOs and the Mindset of Leadership](04%20-%20CEOs%20and%20the%20Mindset%20of%20Leadership.md) — Lee Iacocca vs. Jack Welch, and how one person's mindset becomes a company's culture
5. [Sports Champions: Jordan and McEnroe](05%20-%20Sports%20Champions%20-%20Jordan%20and%20McEnroe.md) — two versions of talent, and only one that fully used it
6. [Mindset in Relationships](06%20-%20Mindset%20in%20Relationships.md) — why some couples read friction as doom and others read it as work
7. [Why Praising Intelligence Backfires](07%20-%20Why%20Praising%20Intelligence%20Backfires.md) — the fifth-grade study on "smart" vs. "worked hard"
8. [Parents, Teachers, and Coaches as Messengers](08%20-%20Parents%2C%20Teachers%2C%20and%20Coaches%20as%20Messengers.md) — Marva Collins, John Wooden, and how adults transmit mindset without meaning to
9. [Changing Your Mindset Deliberately](09%20-%20Changing%20Your%20Mindset%20Deliberately.md) — mindset itself is learnable, and here's how the book argues you build it
10. [Smaller Effects and the Corporate Dilution of Growth Mindset](10%20-%20Smaller%20Effects%20and%20the%20Corporate%20Dilution%20of%20Growth%20Mindset.md) — what the research says now, two decades on
