# The Power of Habit
*Charles Duhigg*

Broken into 11 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — the individual-level mechanics come first, then the same mechanism scales up to organizations and social movements.

## Concepts

1. [The Habit Loop — Cue, Routine, Reward](01%20-%20The%20Habit%20Loop%20-%20Cue%2C%20Routine%2C%20Reward.md) — the three-step mechanism, and the MIT rat-maze study that found it
2. [Eugene Pauly and the Neuroscience of a Habit](02%20-%20Eugene%20Pauly%20and%20the%20Neuroscience%20of%20a%20Habit.md) — the amnesiac patient who proved habits live below memory
3. [The Craving Brain — Pepsodent and Febreze](03%20-%20The%20Craving%20Brain%20-%20Pepsodent%20and%20Febreze.md) — how two ad campaigns manufactured craving from nothing
4. [The Golden Rule of Habit Change](04%20-%20The%20Golden%20Rule%20of%20Habit%20Change.md) — keep the cue and reward, change only the routine
5. [Keystone Habits and the Power of Small Wins](05%20-%20Keystone%20Habits%20and%20the%20Power%20of%20Small%20Wins.md) — why one habit can quietly rewire several others
6. [Paul O'Neill and the Alcoa Turnaround](06%20-%20Paul%20O'Neill%20and%20the%20Alcoa%20Turnaround.md) — the same keystone-habit logic at the scale of a whole company
7. [Willpower as a Trainable Resource — The Starbucks Effect](07%20-%20Willpower%20as%20a%20Trainable%20Resource%20-%20The%20Starbucks%20Effect.md) — why willpower behaves like a muscle, not a fixed trait
8. [Crisis as an Opportunity — How Organizations Rewrite Their Habits](08%20-%20Crisis%20as%20an%20Opportunity%20-%20How%20Organizations%20Rewrite%20Their%20Habits.md) — why the best moment to change institutional habits is right after they fail
9. [Target, Data Mining, and the Habits We Don't Know We Have](09%20-%20Target%2C%20Data%20Mining%2C%20and%20the%20Habits%20We%20Don't%20Know%20We%20Have.md) — how predictable habit shifts became a marketing weapon
10. [Rosa Parks, Strong and Weak Ties, and the Montgomery Bus Boycott](10%20-%20Rosa%20Parks%2C%20Strong%20and%20Weak%20Ties%2C%20and%20the%20Montgomery%20Bus%20Boycott.md) — how a social habit spreads past the people who started it
11. [The Power of Habit and Atomic Habits — Two Books, One Loop](11%20-%20The%20Power%20of%20Habit%20and%20Atomic%20Habits%20-%20Two%20Books%2C%20One%20Loop.md) — same underlying mechanism, narrative case study versus systematized framework
