# Influence
*Robert Cialdini*

Broken into 8 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Six of the chunks map to Cialdini's core "weapons of influence" — the automatic psychological triggers that produce compliance without much conscious deliberation — bookended by a chunk on why the triggers work at all and a closing chunk on how to use the book defensively. Read them in order — later chunks assume the framework from the first.

## Concepts

1. [Weapons of Influence](01%20-%20Weapons%20of%20Influence.md) — why mental shortcuts exist, and how compliance professionals hijack them
2. [Reciprocity](02%20-%20Reciprocity.md) — the debt you feel the instant someone gives you something first
3. [Commitment and Consistency](03%20-%20Commitment%20and%20Consistency.md) — why a small "yes" quietly obligates you to a much bigger one
4. [Social Proof](04%20-%20Social%20Proof.md) — using everyone else's behavior as a substitute for your own judgment
5. [Liking](05%20-%20Liking.md) — why you say yes to people you like, almost regardless of the request
6. [Authority](06%20-%20Authority.md) — deferring to titles, uniforms, and trappings faster than to the substance behind them
7. [Scarcity](07%20-%20Scarcity.md) — why the same thing wanted less feels wanted more once it might disappear
8. [Reading Influence Defensively](08%20-%20Reading%20Influence%20Defensively.md) — closing synthesis, and where this book sits next to *Influence Is Your Superpower*
