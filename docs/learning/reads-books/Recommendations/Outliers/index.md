# Outliers
*Malcolm Gladwell*

Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Gladwell's central claim across all of them: individual success is shaped far more by hidden structural advantages, timing, and cumulative practice than by innate talent alone. Read them in order — later chunks build on earlier ones.

## Concepts

1. [The Matthew Effect](01%20-%20The%20Matthew%20Effect.md) — why Canadian hockey rosters are stacked with kids born in January
2. [The 10,000-Hour Rule](02%20-%20The%2010%2C000-Hour%20Rule.md) — how Bill Gates's 1968 computer access and The Beatles' Hamburg residencies made their practice hours possible
3. [Rethinking the 10,000-Hour Rule](03%20-%20Rethinking%20the%2010%2C000-Hour%20Rule.md) — how far the popular version has drifted from Ericsson's original research, and why that's worth knowing
4. [The IQ Threshold and the Termites](04%20-%20The%20IQ%20Threshold%20and%20the%20Termites.md) — why Terman's "genius" study missed two future Nobel laureates
5. [Practical Intelligence - Chris Langan vs. Robert Oppenheimer](05%20-%20Practical%20Intelligence%20-%20Chris%20Langan%20vs.%20Robert%20Oppenheimer.md) — why knowing how to talk to authority matters as much as raw IQ
6. [Concerted Cultivation and the Parenting Divide](06%20-%20Concerted%20Cultivation%20and%20the%20Parenting%20Divide.md) — Annette Lareau's research on how class shapes whether kids learn to negotiate with institutions
7. [Culture of Honor - Feuds in Kentucky](07%20-%20Culture%20of%20Honor%20-%20Feuds%20in%20Kentucky.md) — how a centuries-old herding economy still shapes Appalachian violence patterns
8. [The Ethnic Theory of Plane Crashes](08%20-%20The%20Ethnic%20Theory%20of%20Plane%20Crashes.md) — how cockpit power distance shaped Korean Air's safety record, and how it was fixed
9. [Rice Paddies and Meaningful Work](09%20-%20Rice%20Paddies%20and%20Meaningful%20Work.md) — why the KIPP Academy bet on more hours, not more aptitude
10. [Outliers vs. Grit - Circumstance or Character?](10%20-%20Outliers%20vs.%20Grit%20-%20Circumstance%20or%20Character.md) — the real, unresolved tension between this book and Duckworth's Grit
