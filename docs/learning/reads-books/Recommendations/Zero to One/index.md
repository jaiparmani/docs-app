# Zero to One
*Peter Thiel*

Broken into 11 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — later chunks assume the earlier ones.

## Concepts

1. [The Contrarian Question](01%20-%20The%20Contrarian%20Question.md) — the "what do you believe that few others do" filter behind the whole book
2. [Definite vs Indefinite Optimism and Pessimism](02%20-%20Definite%20vs%20Indefinite%20Optimism%20and%20Pessimism.md) — why only a specific, planned view of the future produces something new
3. [Monopoly, Not Competition](03%20-%20Monopoly,%20Not%20Competition.md) — why "competition is for losers," and where that claim draws real pushback
4. [The Ideology of Competition](04%20-%20The%20Ideology%20of%20Competition.md) — why fighting over the same goal as everyone else feels like validation but isn't
5. [Last Mover Advantage](05%20-%20Last%20Mover%20Advantage.md) — why being first matters less than being the one still standing decades later
6. [The Power Law of Venture Capital](06%20-%20The%20Power%20Law%20of%20Venture%20Capital.md) — why one outlier bet matters more than a portfolio of safe ones
7. [Secrets — Undiscovered Truths Still Exist](07%20-%20Secrets%20%E2%80%94%20Undiscovered%20Truths%20Still%20Exist.md) — why believing there's something left to find is a precondition for finding it
8. [Founding Teams and the Question of Equity](08%20-%20Founding%20Teams%20and%20the%20Question%20of%20Equity.md) — why co-founder choice and equity design have to happen before the pressure does
9. [The Mafia Principle](09%20-%20The%20Mafia%20Principle.md) — why real culture is who'd want to work together, not what perks are on offer
10. [Sales Is Hidden](10%20-%20Sales%20Is%20Hidden.md) — why distribution matters as much as the product, and why it's built to look invisible
11. [The Founder's Paradox](11%20-%20The%20Founder's%20Paradox.md) — why the traits that build something new are the same ones that can wreck it
