# The Intelligent Investor
*Benjamin Graham*

Broken into 11 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Warren Buffett's mentor's foundational text on value investing — buying below intrinsic value with a margin of safety, and treating the market as a servant rather than a guide. Read them in order — later chunks assume the earlier ones.

## Concepts

1. [Investing vs. Speculating](01%20-%20Investing%20vs.%20Speculating.md) — the foundational distinction the whole book is built on
2. [Mr. Market](02%20-%20Mr.%20Market.md) — the allegory that reframes daily price swings as mood, not information
3. [Margin of Safety](03%20-%20Margin%20of%20Safety.md) — the central unifying concept: never buy without room to be wrong
4. [The Defensive Investor](04%20-%20The%20Defensive%20Investor.md) — the low-effort, rules-based archetype for most people
5. [The Enterprising Investor](05%20-%20The%20Enterprising%20Investor.md) — the higher-effort archetype, and its higher standard of rigor
6. [Diversification for the Defensive Investor](06%20-%20Diversification%20for%20the%20Defensive%20Investor.md) — humility built into the portfolio, not just the analysis
7. [Dollar-Cost Averaging](07%20-%20Dollar-Cost%20Averaging.md) — a formula that removes the timing decision entirely
8. [The Danger of Forecasting the Market](08%20-%20The%20Danger%20of%20Forecasting%20the%20Market.md) — why prediction is a trap, not an edge
9. [Reading the Business Behind the Stock](09%20-%20Reading%20the%20Business%20Behind%20the%20Stock.md) — the concrete financial checks behind every other idea
10. [A Dense, Dated Classic — Read It Annotated](10%20-%20A%20Dense%2C%20Dated%20Classic%20%E2%80%94%20Read%20It%20Annotated.md) — why a modern annotated edition beats the raw original text
11. [The Rigorous Counterpart to The Psychology of Money](11%20-%20The%20Rigorous%20Counterpart%20to%20The%20Psychology%20of%20Money.md) — closing synthesis: Graham's machinery meets Housel's behavior
