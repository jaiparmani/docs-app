# Building a Second Brain

*Tiago Forte*

Broken into 11 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — later chunks assume the earlier ones.

## Concepts

1. [What Is a Second Brain?](01%20-%20What%20Is%20a%20Second%20Brain.md) — why the brain is for having ideas, not storing them
2. [The CODE Framework](02%20-%20The%20CODE%20Framework.md) — the four-step pipeline the rest of the book hangs on
3. [Capture — Keep What Resonates](03%20-%20Capture%20-%20Keep%20What%20Resonates.md) — what's actually worth saving, and what isn't
4. [The Twelve Favorite Problems](04%20-%20The%20Twelve%20Favorite%20Problems.md) — a Feynman technique for directing what you capture
5. [Organize — The PARA Method](05%20-%20Organize%20-%20The%20PARA%20Method.md) — filing by actionability instead of by topic
6. [Distill — Progressive Summarization](06%20-%20Distill%20-%20Progressive%20Summarization.md) — layering a note down to its essence over repeated visits
7. [Express — Show Your Work](07%20-%20Express%20-%20Show%20Your%20Work.md) — why waiting for a finished idea is the wrong instinct
8. [Intermediate Packets](08%20-%20Intermediate%20Packets.md) — the reusable building blocks that make future work faster
9. [Divergence and Convergence](09%20-%20Divergence%20and%20Convergence.md) — the two-beat rhythm underneath all creative work
10. [Project Checklists and the Habits of Digital Organization](10%20-%20Project%20Checklists%20and%20the%20Habits%20of%20Digital%20Organization.md) — the maintenance habits that keep a second brain from rotting
11. [The Second Brain as a Thinking Partner](11%20-%20The%20Second%20Brain%20as%20a%20Thinking%20Partner.md) — closing synthesis of the whole book
