# Same as Ever

*Morgan Housel*

Instead of trying to predict what changes next, Housel studies what never does — fear, greed, the pull of a good story over good data, and the way calm periods quietly breed the next crisis. Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read, drawn from the book's short standalone chapters. This is the middle book of a loose Housel trilogy: *The Psychology of Money* covers behavior and outcomes, *Same as Ever* covers what stays constant underneath the outcomes, and *The Art of Spending Money* narrows in on spending specifically.

## Concepts

1. [Hanging by a Thread](01%20-%20Hanging%20by%20a%20Thread.md) — why history hinges on tiny, unpredictable moments no forecast could catch
2. [Risk Is What You Don't See](02%20-%20Risk%20Is%20What%20You%20Don't%20See.md) — the danger that gets you is by definition the one nobody's discussing
3. [Expectations and Reality](03%20-%20Expectations%20and%20Reality.md) — happiness is a gap, not a number
4. [Best Story Wins](04%20-%20Best%20Story%20Wins.md) — why a compelling narrative beats accurate data almost every time
5. [Calm Plants the Seeds of Crazy](05%20-%20Calm%20Plants%20the%20Seeds%20of%20Crazy.md) — stability is what makes people reckless enough to end it
6. [Too Much, Too Soon, Too Fast](06%20-%20Too%20Much%2C%20Too%20Soon%2C%20Too%20Fast.md) — why growth that skips the slow part breaks first
7. [Overnight Tragedies and Long-Term Miracles](07%20-%20Overnight%20Tragedies%20and%20Long-Term%20Miracles.md) — bad news is sudden, good news is compounding and quiet
8. [Keep Running](08%20-%20Keep%20Running.md) — every edge decays, and success is what plants the decay
9. [Incentives, the Most Powerful Force](09%20-%20Incentives%2C%20the%20Most%20Powerful%20Force.md) — people believe what they're paid, praised, or protected for believing
10. [Wounds Heal, Scars Last](10%20-%20Wounds%20Heal%2C%20Scars%20Last.md) — why two people can live through the same event and never see risk the same way again
