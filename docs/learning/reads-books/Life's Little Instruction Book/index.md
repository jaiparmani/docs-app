# Life's Little Instruction Book

*H. Jackson Brown Jr.*

There is no thesis here to summarize: the book is 511 numbered, mostly single-sentence instructions with no chapters and no connecting argument, and it began as a list Brown compiled for his son leaving for college. Broken into 7 chunks below, each clustering related aphorisms by theme since the source has no chapter structure of its own to follow. Read in any order, or dip in at random — that's closer to how the book is meant to be used than reading it straight through.

## Concepts

1. [A List, Not an Argument](01%20-%20A%20List%2C%20Not%20an%20Argument.md) — why this book has no thesis, and how to actually use it
2. [Punctuality, Promises, and Small Acts of Integrity](02%20-%20Punctuality%2C%20Promises%2C%20and%20Small%20Acts%20of%20Integrity.md) — trust built and lost in the small, unglamorous things
3. [Gratitude, Compliments, and the Rituals of Kindness](03%20-%20Gratitude%2C%20Compliments%2C%20and%20the%20Rituals%20of%20Kindness.md) — noticing people costs nothing and is rarely done
4. [Listening, Forgiving, and Giving the Benefit of the Doubt](04%20-%20Listening%2C%20Forgiving%2C%20and%20Giving%20the%20Benefit%20of%20the%20Doubt.md) — relational habits that keep grudges from accumulating
5. [Doing More Than Is Expected](05%20-%20Doing%20More%20Than%20Is%20Expected.md) — quiet excellence and staying the same person no one's watching
6. [Saving, Avoiding Debt, and Buying Quality](06%20-%20Saving%2C%20Avoiding%20Debt%2C%20and%20Buying%20Quality.md) — a whole philosophy of money in a handful of one-liners
7. [Noticing Beauty and Not Being Owned by Your Possessions](07%20-%20Noticing%20Beauty%20and%20Not%20Being%20Owned%20by%20Your%20Possessions.md) — perspective, curiosity, and keeping things in proportion
