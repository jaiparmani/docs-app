# Man's Search for Meaning
*Viktor E. Frankl*

Broken into 10 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. The first six are drawn from Frankl's own account of imprisonment in Nazi concentration camps, including Auschwitz; the last four set out logotherapy, the theory of meaning he built from that experience. Read them in order — the theory chunks lean on the memoir chunks for their evidence.

## Concepts

1. [The Delusion of Reprieve](01%20-%20The%20Delusion%20of%20Reprieve.md) — how the mind softens catastrophic news just enough to keep functioning
2. [Apathy as Emotional Armor](02%20-%20Apathy%20as%20Emotional%20Armor.md) — why emotional numbness under sustained horror is protection, not moral failure
3. [The Last of the Human Freedoms](03%20-%20The%20Last%20of%20the%20Human%20Freedoms.md) — the one form of control that survives when everything else is taken
4. [A Why to Live For](04%20-%20A%20Why%20to%20Live%20For.md) — why losing a future purpose was more dangerous than physical deprivation
5. [Love as the Ultimate Goal](05%20-%20Love%20as%20the%20Ultimate%20Goal.md) — how an inner relationship sustained meaning independent of the beloved's fate
6. [The Danger After Liberation](06%20-%20The%20Danger%20After%20Liberation.md) — the psychological risk that arrives once the crisis is actually over
7. [The Will to Meaning](07%20-%20The%20Will%20to%20Meaning.md) — Frankl's rebuttal to Freud's pleasure and Adler's power
8. [Three Roads to Meaning: Work, Love, Suffering](08%20-%20Three%20Roads%20to%20Meaning%20-%20Work%2C%20Love%2C%20Suffering.md) — the three avenues logotherapy says are never all closed at once
9. [Tragic Optimism](09%20-%20Tragic%20Optimism.md) — saying yes to life without denying pain, guilt, or death
10. [The Existential Vacuum](10%20-%20The%20Existential%20Vacuum.md) — Frankl's diagnosis of meaninglessness as a modern, not just a wartime, affliction
