# Atomic Habits
*James Clear*

Broken into 12 concepts, one per chunk, each a 3-5 minute read. Each chunk covers the core idea, why it matters, a specific example from the book, a practical way to apply it, and a question to sit with. Read them in order — later chunks assume the earlier ones.

## Concepts

1. [The Habit Loop](01%20-%20The%20Habit%20Loop.md) — the four-step mechanism every habit runs on
2. [Habits Are Votes for Identity](02%20-%20Habits%20Are%20Votes%20for%20Identity.md) — why identity-based change outlasts outcome-based change
3. [The Plateau of Latent Potential](03%20-%20The%20Plateau%20of%20Latent%20Potential.md) — why habits feel pointless right up until they don't
4. [The Four Laws Framework](04%20-%20The%20Four%20Laws%20Framework.md) — the organizing structure the rest of the book hangs on
5. [Law 1 — Make It Obvious](05%20-%20Law%201%20-%20Make%20It%20Obvious.md) — cueing the behavior you actually want
6. [Law 2 — Make It Attractive](06%20-%20Law%202%20-%20Make%20It%20Attractive.md) — engineering craving, not just willpower
7. [Law 3 — Make It Easy](07%20-%20Law%203%20-%20Make%20It%20Easy.md) — friction is the real variable, not motivation
8. [Law 4 — Make It Satisfying](08%20-%20Law%204%20-%20Make%20It%20Satisfying.md) — why habits need an immediate payoff to survive
9. [The Goldilocks Rule](09%20-%20The%20Goldilocks%20Rule.md) — staying engaged once novelty wears off
10. [Inverting the Laws to Break Bad Habits](10%20-%20Inverting%20the%20Laws%20to%20Break%20Bad%20Habits.md) — the same four laws, run backward
11. [Standards Over Goals](11%20-%20Standards%20Over%20Goals.md) — why systems outlast the goals that inspired them
12. [Identity Change as the Deepest Layer](12%20-%20Identity%20Change%20as%20the%20Deepest%20Layer.md) — closing synthesis of the whole book
